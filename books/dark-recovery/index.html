Core frame

The goal is not endless self-control. The goal is environmental inevitability: fewer exposed moments where drinking becomes the easy path.

Operating loop

Contain, stabilize, expose, integrate, loosen. A cyclical process for adjusting the system as life changes.

Designed for honesty

The guide asks for accurate data: timing, triggers, energy state, social context, and the role alcohol has been playing.

Stop organizing your life around the thing you are trying to leave.

Most recovery approaches keep alcohol as the protagonist. Every rule, check-in, and identity statement still points back to the substance.

Dark Recovery shifts the center of gravity. The question becomes: what does your life need in order to operate without alcohol as a load-bearing component?

That means defaults. Friction. Visibility. Time structure. Identity cues. The book is direct, practical, and intentionally unsentimental.

Use it when

Willpower has been unreliable and you need a system that assumes fatigue, stress, boredom, and ambivalence will show up.

Expect

Short chapters, operational language, and concrete audits you can run without turning recovery into a performance.

Not this

This is not a medical detox guide, therapy replacement, or spiritual recovery program. It is one useful operating model.

If alcohol withdrawal may be medically risky for you, get qualified medical help. This book is educational and practical; it is not emergency care.
