You probably do not need a bigger website plan.

You need one useful page that explains what you do, gives the right person a reason to care, and makes the next step obvious.

Most unfinished pages are not blocked by design or technology. They are blocked by unresolved decisions, fear of being judged, and an oversized idea of what the page has to accomplish.

This book helps you make those decisions, write the plain version first, and publish something that can finally do its job.

What the book helps you do

  • Choose the one page worth finishing now
  • Give that page one primary job
  • Define the audience, offer, outcome, proof, and next step
  • Write clear copy before trying to sound impressive
  • Build the minimum useful page without making it skeletal
  • Add enough proof to support the claims
  • Remove practical reasons a qualified visitor might hesitate
  • Publish before confidence arrives
  • Improve the page from evidence instead of mood
