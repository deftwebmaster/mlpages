    Help

    • Move the deflector with arrow keys, A/D, mouse, or drag on mobile.
    • Press Space (or tap Launch) to launch the orb.
    • Where the orb strikes the deflector changes its rebound angle.
    • Break reactor components to release energy.
    • Collect energy with the deflector and route it into Deflector, Orb, or Reactor Control.
    • Press Space to activate the selected channel's ability once charged.
    • Hold Shift (or tap Catch) to magnetically catch and reposition the orb.
    • Complete the primary objective to breach the chamber.
