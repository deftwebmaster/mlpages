How to Play

Move

Swipe anywhere on the board, or use WASD / arrow keys. Your next turn is buffered, so you can steer into a corner early.

Drain every node

Energy nodes are worth 10 each. Clear the last one and the sector ends immediately — the clock is your real opponent.

Read the drones

Hunters take the shortest path to you. Interceptors aim ahead of you. Sentinels patrol until you come close. Trackers predict your escape. Wanderers ignore all of that.

Power modules

Eight seconds of vulnerability, plus 10% speed. Chained drones score 100 → 200 → 400 → 800 → 1600.

Grid Shift

Touch a violet terminal to rotate a junction, slide a wall, toggle a gate, extend a bridge or drop a barrier. One shared 15 second cooldown — the real skill is deciding when not to spend it.

Secrets & ranks

Amber seams mark sealed compartments; some terminals breach them for +500. Rank blends time, deaths and secrets — a Perfect Clear needs no deaths, every secret, and the target time.

No game over

Deaths cost you time and rank, never the run. Respawn is instant and retries are unlimited.
