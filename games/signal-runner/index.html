      SETTINGS

      HOW TO PLAY

      • Move one cell

        Swipe, use the arrow keys or WASD, or tap a neighbouring cell. You move a single cell at a time — the network does not.

      • Avoid security packets

        Orange traffic is lethal to any frequency. Wait in the gaps; there is no penalty for patience.

      • Ride data platforms

        Dark rows have no floor. Land on a moving bar and it carries you. Being carried off the edge ends the attempt.

      • Switch frequency

        Tap the FREQ button or press Space. Cyan is marked with a circle, violet with a diamond. Matching frequency passes safely through a hazard, gate or platform; the opposite does not.

      • Read the scan cycle

        Scanners always charge before they fire. A hatched area with a filling bar is a warning, not a hit.

      • Reach every uplink

        Activate a terminal at the top and you return to the start to fetch the next. Activated uplinks stay online even if you are lost on the way back.
