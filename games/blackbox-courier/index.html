HOW TO PLAY

  • DRAG TO STEER. Touch anywhere in the tunnel and slide. On desktop use ← → or A D, or drag with the mouse.
  • HOLD PHASE to cross violet energy barriers. Hold the PHASE button, press Space, or hold a second finger anywhere on the tunnel.
  • PHASE DRAINS ENERGY and costs a little payload stability. It recharges whenever you are not phased.
  • SOLID HAZARDS STAY SOLID. Debris, gates, rotors and mine cores end the run — phase never passes through them.
  • COLLECT DATA FRAGMENTS for score and multiplier. Green repair nodes and calibration gates restore payload stability.
  • NEAR MISSES PAY. Skim a lethal hazard without touching it for bonus score and multiplier.
  • CLEAR A SECTION CLEANLY — no damage, no corruption, no wall contact, most fragments taken — for CLEAN TRANSIT.
  • CHECKPOINTS every 30 seconds restore stability and briefly ease the speed.

HAZARD LEGEND

  • Solid debris and gates — lethal
  • Energy barrier — phase through it
  • Mine — lethal core, damaging ring
  • Corruption field — drains payload
  • Calibration gate — restores payload
  • Data fragment — score
  • Repair node — restores payload
