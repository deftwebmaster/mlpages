Conflict overview

Business disputes rarely begin in the courtroom.

They begin with a broken agreement, a shifting relationship, an emerging threat, or a decision that can no longer be postponed. Sterling Kane helps clients identify the real point of pressure before the dispute defines the business.

Strategic statement

The objective is not litigation for its own sake. The objective is control.

Some disputes should be resolved early. Others require immediate court intervention. Sterling Kane prepares for both without confusing activity for progress.
