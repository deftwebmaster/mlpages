Northbeam Arc

$89

Northbeam Arc provides focused distance lighting and broad task illumination in a compact wearable format.

The adjustable front housing allows the beam to be directed without shifting the entire headband. A secure three-point strap system distributes weight evenly, while large controls remain usable with gloves.

Arc is designed for repairs, hiking, power outages, job-site work, and any task that requires both hands.

Portable Area Light

Northbeam Station

$139

Northbeam Station provides broad, even illumination without the harsh glare of a single exposed light source.

Its impact-resistant diffuser, stable base, reinforced handle, and large control dial make it suitable for home outages, campsites, workshops, patios, and temporary work areas.

Station can also provide emergency power to a mobile device through its protected USB output.
