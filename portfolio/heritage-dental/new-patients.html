New patients

Your first visit, made clearer.

Start with what concerns you most, then review findings, priorities, options, and estimated costs before scheduling treatment.

What to Bring

  • Photo identification
  • Insurance information if applicable
  • Medication list
  • Relevant dental records
  • Questions or concerns
  • Information about current symptoms

What to Expect

  • Conversation about concerns
  • Health-history review
  • Examination
  • Necessary imaging
  • Discussion of findings
  • Treatment priorities
  • Cost estimate for recommended care
