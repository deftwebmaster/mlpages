Emergency dentistry

When a dental concern should not wait.

Emergency appointments focus on identifying the source of pain or damage, reducing immediate risk, and explaining the available next steps.

Call emergency services for severe facial trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, difficulty breathing, or other life-threatening symptoms.

Common concerns

  • Severe tooth pain
  • Broken tooth
  • Lost crown or filling
  • Dental swelling
  • Injury to a tooth
  • Pain while biting
  • Sudden sensitivity
  • Concern after recent treatment
