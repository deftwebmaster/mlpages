Services / Clear Aligners

Clear aligners

Straighter teeth without traditional brackets.

Clear aligner treatment uses a planned series of removable trays to address selected spacing, crowding, and alignment concerns.

Planning steps

Candidate evaluation

Review alignment concerns, bite needs, dental health, and patient goals.

Digital scan

Capture a selected case without traditional impression material.

Treatment preview

Discuss what the plan may address and what alternatives exist.

Tray sequence

Use removable trays according to the planned sequence.

Progress visits

Monitor movement and comfort at regular intervals.

Retention planning

Discuss retainers and maintenance after active treatment.

Eligibility and results are not guaranteed. This page describes a fictional service model for portfolio demonstration.
