Services / Preventive Care

Preventive care

Protect what is healthy.

Preventive dental care focuses on detecting concerns early, maintaining gum health, reducing avoidable treatment, and creating a realistic plan for home care.

Services may include

  • Comprehensive examinations
  • Routine examinations
  • Professional cleanings
  • Digital X-rays
  • Gum-health assessments
  • Oral-cancer screening
  • Fluoride treatment
  • Nightguard evaluation

Routine visits should feel useful.

The goal is to understand what is healthy, what needs attention, and what daily habits can support long-term comfort.
